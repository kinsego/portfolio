Project Title One

Identity & Print — 2025

The problem

A short paragraph naming who had this problem, why it mattered to them, and what was broken or missing before you got involved. Ground it in a real situation, not the brief you were handed.

The process

Two or three sentences on what you learned during research or discovery that shaped the direction — and a key decision point where you had real options, with the reasoning for what you chose.

Include the pivot here — what didn't work, and what you learned from it. This is usually the most compelling part of the story.

Constraints

Name the real-world limits — time, budget, technical constraints, stakeholder pushback — and how you navigated them. This is often more impressive to a reader than the final visual alone.

The outcome

What changed for the user or business, with real metrics if you have them. Close with a brief reflection — what you'd do differently, or what you learned.
